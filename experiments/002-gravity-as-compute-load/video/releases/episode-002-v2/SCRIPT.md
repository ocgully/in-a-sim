> In a game, a region full of stuff costs more to compute, so it updates slower. What if that's what mass does? The slowdown spreads outward, and fades with distance, in exactly the shape Newton found for gravity. This idea isn't new. Brian Whitworth and Alagoz suggested it years ago. We wanted to build it and see if it holds up.
